# v1.0.4 — Partners aim where they look

Player report: "Shooting makes it look straight up. Doing anything else makes it look straight down."

A partner's body takes its up/down aim as -1..1, and the mod gave it degrees - so a partner looking a little
down had the body staring at the floor, and the couple of degrees of recoil when firing threw the arms straight
up over the head (and they stayed there until the partner looked elsewhere). Now the body aims the way the
player looks: level when looking ahead, a bit down or up when they look down or up, steady while shooting.
Melee and reloading keep the body upright, as in the game.

Checked on both screens (the host seeing a partner and a partner seeing the host): looking 30 down, ahead,
30 up, and firing with recoil.
