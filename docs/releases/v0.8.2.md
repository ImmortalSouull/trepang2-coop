# v0.8.2 — mission weapon and gas mask for friends

- **Mission weapon:** in a mission the host starts with its mission weapon (from the checkpoint), a friend had
  only the pistol. Now a joining friend finds the same weapon at their feet with a notice - hold F to take it.
- **Gas mask** (KillGhosts and other gas areas): when the host's mask goes on or off, it does for every friend
  too (and for their bodies on the host).
