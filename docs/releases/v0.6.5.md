# v0.6.5 — your safehouse look, seen by everyone

- Players now look **exactly as they dressed themselves in the safehouse** — the game's own outfits (106,
  Patriarch, 107 and the rest you've unlocked) and the customization: jacket, gloves, pants and shoes colours
  and camo. Change it at the safehouse station (hold F) like in single player; partners see it within a
  couple of seconds.
- The jacket and gloves colours are also shown on the body (the base game only shows them on your
  first-person arms).
- `look=106` in config.txt puts everyone in the Light 106 suit instead (the v0.6.2 look).
