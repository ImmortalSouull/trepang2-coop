# v0.7.3 — new co-op menu, every enemy as a look, look preview

- **New F4 menu** in the game's own style (like the pause menu): dark see-through tiles with a thin grey
  edge, white caps text, the tile under the mouse lights up. Sections: session, players, network, look.
  The panel scales to fit the screen (it was cut off at 720p), long names no longer spill out of the buttons.
- **F5 / F6 / F7 / F8 are gone** - everything (host, next arena, join, leave) is in the F4 menu.
- **Every enemy as a look:** about 330 enemy variants in 30 factions, found in the game's files - Mall Cops,
  Cultists, Horizon guards / elite / **TTMF (night vision)** / Black Ops, Hazmats, Bad Oil bandits, Lawyer
  Thugs, Replicas, 107, bodyguards and more. Arrows pick the faction and the variant.
- **Look preview:** next to the menu your chosen look turns slowly on a neutral grey background, lit by its
  own lamps - exactly what the others will see.
- **Fix:** enemies on the client were stuck in a falling pose (their copies had no movement mode). They
  stand, walk and run normally now.
- **Fix:** grenade warning circles - a partner's body drew them on your screen too, so you got circles for
  grenades next to your partner and one grenade showed one circle per player. Now only your own.
- **Fix:** the host's copy of a partner's enemy outfit was sent back to the partner as an extra enemy.
