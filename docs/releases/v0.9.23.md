# v0.9.23 — Partner crash on checkpoint reload fixed

- **Fix: the partner's game sometimes crashed when the host reloaded a checkpoint** (death, "load last
  checkpoint") or when joining a mission in progress. Their copy of every door was opened or locked in the same
  moment the level was still setting up. Doors are now matched to the host's a few seconds after the partner is
  in. KillMothman: 2 crashes in 8 checkpoint reloads before, 0 in 15 after.
- The host no longer keeps sending the looks of players who already left (it grew with every rejoin).
- Checked in co-op: the Safehouse betrayal scene and the move on to the next mission; loading a checkpoint in
  KillMothman.
