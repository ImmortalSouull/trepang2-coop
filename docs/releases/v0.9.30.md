# v0.9.30 — Partner crash after mission end fixed

- **Fix: the partner's game crashed coming back after a mission end** (and now and then after checkpoint loads).
  When the host's level took long to load, the partner's mod gave up waiting and went back to the menu to try
  again - but the first attempt still went through, and the game ended up in the host's level with the mod not
  acting as a partner; seconds later it crashed. The mod now recognises that it is connected after all.
  Mission end -> safehouse with a partner: crashed in nearly every try while recording (slow loads), 0 in 11
  tries after.
- Bodies of other players on a partner's screen can no longer be switched on to "think" by a level script
  (another crash on joining).
- The safehouse arrival scene waits for partners who were in the mission with the host and plays for everyone
  from the start (it used to start before they were back).
- A partner joins a running cutscene a moment after their own body is in (checked: joining the Safehouse
  betrayal mid-scene).
- The game's own end-of-level triggers aren't repeated on partners any more (their game loaded the next level
  by itself and had to reconnect).
- New developer tool: tools/dumpstack.py unwinds a crash dump's stack without symbols.
