# v0.4.0 — the partner's bullets really count

## Fixed (important)
- **The partner's shots now actually damage enemies on the host.** Before this version the
  partner's bullets only hit the partner's own copies of enemies, which can't take damage in
  Unreal's networking, so most of the partner's shooting did nothing for the team.
  Now every shot the partner fires is re-traced by the host from the partner's camera and the
  weapon's damage is applied there (shotgun pellets included).
- **Headshots** from the partner use the weapon's headshot multiplier (one pistol headshot kills a cultist).
- Partner kills keep earning Horde money.

## Also
- Roadmap to v1.0.0 (full co-op story campaign) published in PLAN.md.

## Known issues
- The partner's grenades, kicks and sword don't damage enemies on the host yet (next update).
- Weapon spread for the partner is simplified (straight from the camera; shotgun pellets use a 4° cone).
- A "NONE" friendly-name label when the host aims at a partner.
- Internet play needs UDP port 7777 open on the host (or Porthole / Radmin VPN / ZeroTier).
- Story missions aren't supported yet.
