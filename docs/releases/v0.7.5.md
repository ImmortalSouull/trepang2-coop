# v0.7.5 — partners' actions on their bodies

- **Reload, weapon melee strike, grenade throw and weapon switch are now shown on the partner's body**
  (and on the host's body for partners). Each weapon has the game's own third-person animation for these;
  the mod plays it on the other players' bodies the moment the arms start it - also the strike on enemies
  lying on the ground, and with an enemy look on (the outfit follows the body).
