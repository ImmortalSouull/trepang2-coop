# v0.9.6 — faster rejoin after level changes

- **Back in faster after a level change:** partners now retry joining every 6 seconds (first try after 4) instead
  of every 15, so they're back right after the host's level is up. Measured on a level restart: 14 s instead of
  18-20 s (KillCultists), 14-16 s on KillMothman.
- The "host doesn't answer" notice now appears only when it really takes long (not during every level change).
