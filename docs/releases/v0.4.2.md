# v0.4.2

- **The partner's frag grenades now damage enemies on the host** (with falloff from the center),
  and grenade kills earn Horde money. The partner's game reports where its grenade exploded and the
  host applies the damage there. Players are not hurt by the partner's grenades.
- Verified with test explosions; please report if a real thrown grenade doesn't count.

Known issues: the partner's kick and sword don't damage enemies on the host yet; a "NONE" label when
the host aims at a partner; internet play needs UDP port 7777 (or Porthole / Radmin VPN / ZeroTier).
