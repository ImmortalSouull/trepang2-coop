# v0.9.22 — Freezing enemy looks left out, LOOK preview crash fixed

- **Enemy looks that froze the game are left out:** 28 of the 328 enemy looks (Hazmat, SSF, oil rig workers,
  some TF63 soldiers and a few others) froze the game forever while loading. Every look was checked, each
  suspect again in a fresh game. Those 28 are no longer in the LOOK list; if you had one picked, you get your own
  outfit back. If a look ever freezes the game anyway, the mod turns it off by itself on the next start.
- **Fix: both games crashed after opening the F4 LOOK tab ~7 times in co-op.** The look preview is now paused
  when the menu closes instead of being rebuilt every time. All 300 remaining looks were tested in co-op (both
  players wear the look, open LOOK, walk and shoot): no freezes; one crash in 300 that did not happen again
  when that look was retested 3 times.
- Look-only enemy figures (costumes, the preview) keep their guns hidden instead of deleting them.
