# v0.2.1 — first public alpha

First playable build: co-op in Horde (Survival) mode.

## What works
- Host a Horde arena (F5, pick arena with F8), friend joins by IP (F6), leave with F7.
- Each player plays their own full local character (first-person gun, HUD); the host sees the partner.
- Shots and damage to enemies from both players; enemy deaths are synced.
- Enemies hurt the partner too (damage to the partner's body on the host is forwarded).
- Horde HUD (wave / enemies left / money / timer) for the partner.
- Down instead of death: at ≤30% HP you go down, a partner next to you revives you in 3 s.
  Everyone down → the arena restarts and partners reconnect automatically.
- The host's pause menu doesn't freeze the partner; a level restart by the game re-hosts automatically.

## Known issues
- The host always sees the partner holding a pistol (weapon switching isn't synced).
- Horde shop / money works for the host only.
- A "NONE" friendly-name label over the partner on the host.
- Internet play needs UDP port 7777 open on the host (or Porthole / Radmin VPN / ZeroTier).
- Story missions aren't supported yet.
