# v0.6.1 — proper player models

- **Partners now look like full soldiers.** The host used to appear to friends as half a body (legs only)
  with a pistol floating in the air; now everyone sees the whole third-person character holding their gun.
- **Partners walk.** Bodies play walking / strafing animations as players move, instead of sliding frozen
  in one pose.
- Removed floating extra guns (a friend's own hidden copy and a duplicate gun on the host's body).
- The "NONE / NONE - 0" box no longer shows when you look at your partner (their marker shows the name).
- Animations are basic: no crouch, jump or melee poses for partners yet.
