# v0.8.1 — side missions, menus stop the controls

- **Side missions in co-op** (Cavern and the others): the host starts one as usual and presses F4 -> HOST,
  friends join like in the campaign.
- **Fix:** a friend could appear inside a wall or outside the map when joining. Now the mod looks for a free
  spot next to the host (behind or beside them, with floor under it).
- **Fix:** with the Esc menu or the F4 menu open you could still walk, look around and shoot (the game can't
  pause in co-op). Now your controls stop while a menu is open.
- **Fix:** after joining, the partner sometimes had the game's menu stuck on screen.
