# v0.9.9 — invisibility in co-op

- **Invisibility works in co-op:** a partner's invisibility (E) only hid them on their own screen - the
  enemies (which live on the host) kept seeing and shooting them, and others saw them as usual. Now the
  partner's body on the host turns invisible too (so the enemies lose them) and everyone sees them disappear -
  body, enemy look and gun. The host's invisibility shows for partners as well.
