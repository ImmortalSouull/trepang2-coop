# v0.9.25 — Partners who fall off the map come back

- **A partner who falls off the map is brought back to the host.** Falling through the floor or off an edge
  used to leave the partner in a white void (the game removes a player who falls out of the world), and
  pressing TO THE HOST then crashed their game. Now a long fall (10 s) or getting close to the bottom of the
  world moves them next to the host, and if the game already removed their body they get a new one there.
- Checked in co-op on every campaign map (Prologue, Cultists, Ghosts, Syndicate, Horizon, Mothman, Safehouse):
  the partner gets in, then 3 level restarts in a row - no crashes.
