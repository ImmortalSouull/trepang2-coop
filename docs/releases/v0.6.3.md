# v0.6.3 — partner animations

Every player's moves are now mirrored on the partner's body (checked from both sides):
- walking and strafing, **sprinting**, **crouching** and walking crouched, **sliding**, **jumping**, dropkick;
- **looking up and down** — the body raises or lowers its gun with the player's view;
- the grip matches the weapon (pistol, rifle...);
- **dual wielding** — a second gun appears in the partner's left hand (the pose stays two-handed: the game
  has no third-person dual-wield animation, so the guns are close together);
- **the host's shots are now seen and heard by friends** (before only the friends' shots reached the host).

Not shown on the partner's body yet: reloading and the melee strike on the ground (those are first-person
arm animations only).
