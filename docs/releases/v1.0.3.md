# v1.0.3 — Smoother over VPN / internet, no more ghost enemies

For "works great on LAN, laggy and out of sync over VPN" and "extremely buggy":

- **Partners move smoothly on the host's screen.** Over a VPN or the internet a partner's position arrives
  unevenly, in bursts, and their body jumped from spot to spot (and stood frozen in between). The host now
  glides it to the latest position. Measured with a simulated bumpy connection: frames where the partner
  stood frozen went from 82 of 129 to 0.
- **Your hits count.** A partner's shot was checked again on the host, where the enemies stand slightly
  elsewhere because of the network delay - shots that clearly hit on the partner's screen could miss. Now the
  partner's game also says what it hit, and the host gives that enemy the damage when its own check misses.
- **No more ghost enemies.** An enemy killed far away from a partner could later show up on that partner's
  screen alive - standing there for good, impossible to kill. Partners' games now compare their enemies with
  the host's every second and put such ghosts down.
- **Fixed: the host could freeze or crash right after a level loaded** (rare: 3 times in a night of testing).
  The mod touched the game's startup screen in the first seconds after a load, while the loading movie was
  still handing over - it now waits like it already did for the key press. 20 host starts in a row: 0.

Checked: big test passes with 4, 3 and 2 players (every campaign map, Horde arenas, side missions),
partners joining one by one in a campaign mission, a partner's game killed and coming back, partner damage
with clear shots (13 of 13 hit), 12 host starts with 4 players (no freeze).
