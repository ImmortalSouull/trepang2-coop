# v0.3.0 — full Horde loop for the partner

## New
- **Weapons on the floor for the partner**: guns dropped by enemies, level guns and guns the host buys
  can now be picked up by the partner (the partner gets a working copy; whoever takes a gun first gets it).
- **Partner kills earn Horde money** for the team (before, only the host's kills paid).
- **Partner markers**: an objective icon with distance over every other player; on the host it says
  "PARTNER - DOWN" when that player is down.
- **On-screen notices** for co-op events: you are down, your partner is down, reviving N%, back on your feet.
- Tested with **3 players** (host + 2).

## Shop
The Horde shop works through the host: the host buys, bought guns drop at the shop and either player
can pick them up.

## Known issues
- A "NONE" friendly-name label when the host aims at a partner.
- Ammo boxes / health pickups are per player (each player has their own copy of the arena's pickups).
- Partner weapon pickup is verified by sync tests; please report if picking up with F fails.
- Internet play needs UDP port 7777 open on the host (or Porthole / Radmin VPN / ZeroTier).
- Story missions aren't supported yet.
