# v1.0.1 — Friends can join any time (3-4 players)

**Fixed: a 3rd or 4th player joining while another partner was already playing could never get in.**
Their game connected, loaded the level, and was dropped about ten seconds later - again and again. Joining
together at the start (how all the 1.0.0 test passes joined) and two players worked, which is why it slipped
through.

Why: the mod's messages from a partner reach the host through the engine's "change name" call, and the
engine answered every one of them with a "player changed name" notice to *all* players. A partner already
playing sends about 40 messages a second, so a newcomer still loading the level was buried in notices it
couldn't acknowledge yet, and its connection overflowed. Those notices are now skipped, and the host also
keeps what it has for a newcomer (spawn point, campaign progress) until the newcomer's game is in the level.

Checked: partners joining one after another (25 s apart) with 3 and 4 players - nobody dropped, all names
shown in F4; 4 players joining together + a level restart; a campaign mission with 4 players (elevator,
restart, mission end -> safehouse).
