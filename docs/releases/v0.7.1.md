# v0.7.1 — cutscenes wait for everyone

- When a cutscene is about to start, it now **waits until every player has come to where it starts**.
  Meanwhile everybody sees "WAITING FOR PLAYERS 1/2" (the count follows how many play) at the top of the
  screen and a "GATHER HERE" marker on the spot, and keeps playing normally. When the last player arrives,
  the cutscene plays for everyone at once and the level goes on.
- Cutscenes at the start of a level also wait for the players who are still reconnecting after the level
  change.
- The text is in Russian when the game is in Russian.
