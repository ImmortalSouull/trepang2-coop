# v1.0.0 — The whole campaign in co-op, up to 4 players

**Trepang2 Co-op 1.0** - play the whole Trepang2 campaign and Horde together, **up to 4 players**.

What 1.0 means: every campaign map, every Horde arena and every side mission has been played in co-op, with joins,
level restarts, checkpoint reloads, mission ends and the credits - and the whole mod was run through big automated
test passes with 1, 2, 3 and 4 players (one game copy per player). No known bug blocks a play-through. Boss fights
are shared like every other enemy (health bars, damage from every player), but their special scripted scenes have
not been played start to finish in co-op yet - please report how they go.

Big test passes on the final build (each: F4/F6 menus, EXTRAS, enemy looks, fighting with partner damage,
down / revive / spectating / everyone down, 10 Horde arenas, every campaign map with join + level restart +
checkpoint reload, elevator ride + fall rescue, mission end -> safehouse, the credits, all 7 side missions):
- 1 player: 101/101 checks OK
- 2 players: 107/108 + Horde re-check 23/23
- 3 players: 114/115
- 4 players: 119/121 + Horde re-check 30/32
No crashes and no Lua errors in any of the final passes; every failed check was traced to the test script
itself (clicks to an unfocused window, a test spot off the map, a newcomer's name arriving a second late).
Also: 30+ host starts with 4 game copies without a freeze, 5 rounds of "everyone down -> arena restart" with 4
players, 4 level restarts and all 7 side missions with 4 players.

Fixed in this build (found by those passes):
- Up to 4 players: the 4th player was dropped again and again a few seconds after joining. The mod sent every
  player's animation state as its own network message (dozens per second to every partner) - a partner still
  loading the level overflowed its connection. Now one message for everyone, and partners get broadcasts only
  once they're in.
- The host could freeze at "hosting" / after a level load (engine deadlock while the loading screen's movie
  stops). The mod no longer dismisses the loading screen in its first seconds or presses again while it closes.
- A partner crashed arriving in the safehouse (bodies re-dressed while the level was still setting up).
- Partners could crash while reconnecting after a restart / travelling to a side mission (the mod dismissed
  the NEXT loading screen while it was still starting) - only the current level's loading screen is dismissed now.
- Crash when pressing JOIN and opening the F4 LOOK tab while still connecting.
- Extra player bodies after loading a checkpoint (the save kept the partners' bodies) - the host removes them.
- F4 player list fits 4 players and no longer lists players who left; spectating while down goes through every
  partner (left click: next partner ... your own view), standing partners first.
- A partner's hidden helper body no longer shows up after scenes (Prologue intro, safehouse).

The road to 1.0 (0.9.22-0.9.30): enemy looks that froze the game left out, LOOK preview crash, partner crash on
checkpoint reload, the end of the game, rescue when falling off the map, elevators / roll-up doors / laser gates
for everyone, partners told when the host leaves, host crash on level changes, partner crash after a mission end.

Known: Steam mode - one report of the joining player stuck on the title screen (can't be reproduced with one
Steam account yet); IP mode with Radmin VPN / ZeroTier works meanwhile. A UE4SS.log from the joining player helps.
