# v1.0.5 — Your guns carry over, enemies talk for everyone

From player reports (thanks, Golden1Wolf):

- **Partners keep the guns they actually have.** The game carries your weapons to the next level, but it only
  stores them in its own level-change code - which runs on the host's game, not a partner's. So a partner arrived
  with the gun from their last mission end (or last combat simulator run), whatever they picked up since. The
  mod now keeps a partner's loadout up to date while they play.
- **Partners hear the enemies.** Enemy voice lines (callouts, taunts, panic) only played on the host. Now a
  partner hears them too, from their own game files - so voice-over mods (like a mallcop VO mod) work for
  partners as well.

Checked: a partner's stored loadout follows what they hold, and after a level change they spawn with exactly
the stored loadout; enemy lines in Horde (12 sent, all played on the partner, 9 different sounds); big test
pass with 2 players.
