# v0.9.29 — Level change crashes fixed

- **Fix: the host's game sometimes crashed right after a level change** with a partner in (mission end -> safehouse,
  next mission, checkpoint loads). A message the partner sent just before the change was handled after it,
  pointing at the partner's old, already removed player. Such leftovers are now dropped, and the mod waits
  for the new level while the old one is being torn down. Mission end -> safehouse -> mission again: 2 host
  crashes in 3 rounds before, 0 in 8 rounds (16 level changes) after.
- **Fix: the partner's game sometimes crashed on joining a level** (seen after a mission end). The interaction
  states and the current mission the host sends a newcomer are applied a few seconds after the partner is in.
- Checked in co-op for 1.0: all 50 Horde arenas, all 7 side missions, every campaign map, long fights - no
  crashes.
