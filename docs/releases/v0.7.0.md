# v0.7.0 — campaign: cutscenes, dialogue, doors

- **Cutscenes for everyone.** When a cutscene starts on the host, every player sees the same cutscene at the
  same moment (kept in step to about a tenth of a second). Partners are not written into the scene; after
  it, everyone gets control back next to the host. Joining in the middle of a cutscene starts it from there.
- **Radio and dialogue lines** (with the speaker's portrait and subtitles) are heard by all players, once.
- **Doors**: doors the host's story locks or unlocks are locked/unlocked for everyone, and doors that open on
  the host open for everyone - no more partners stuck behind a door only the host could open.
- Still to come: hostages, terminals / hacking, objective markers, elevators.
