# v0.8.0 — boss health bars for everyone, enemy health in the menu

- **Boss health bars for every player.** The game shows a boss's health bar only on the host; now partners
  get the same bar (name, stages) and it goes down with the boss's health.
- **ENEMY HEALTH in the F4 menu:** how much tougher enemies get for every extra player - from +0% to +200%
  (default +50%). The host's setting counts; the menu shows the multiplier right now.
- **Fix:** the partner's HUD showed a white health bar and the ammo counter didn't go down (it was bound to
  the wrong body).
- **Fix:** orange hit arcs - hits on the partner showed on the host's screen (so the host saw arcs twice) and
  the partner saw none. Now each player sees the arcs for their own hits.
